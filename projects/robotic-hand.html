Robotic hand

A 3D-printed, tendon-driven hand with 11 servos, controlled in Python on an NVIDIA Jetson Nano. Built for undergraduate research at Iowa State.

Timeline
Aug 2024 to May 2025
Actuation
11 servos, tendon driven
Controller
Jetson Nano, Python
Design
Open-source Robot Nano Hand

What it is

The hand follows the open-source Robot Nano Hand design from the NVIDIA Jetson community. Servos pull fishing line tendons that run through the printed fingers, which keeps motor mass out of the fingers so they stay light.

My part

I built the hand and wrote the Python motion control on the Jetson Nano. Each servo is controlled individually, and coordinating them produced grasping, light throwing and other motions.
